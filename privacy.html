Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how uses the personal information you give us.

What we collect

When you order, request a quote or send a message, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email (if given), delivery address and GhanaPost GPS address, and what you are ordering.

How we use it

  • to confirm, prepare and deliver your order and send your invoice
  • to answer your questions and quote requests
  • to handle returns and warranty claims

We do not sell or rent your information, and we only share it with delivery partners when needed to deliver your order.

WhatsApp, email and payments

Orders and messages are sent through WhatsApp or your email app, which have their own privacy policies. If online payment is enabled, card and mobile-money payments are handled by Paystack; we never see or store your card details.

Your cart

Your cart is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. Nothing is sent to us until you place an order.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).
