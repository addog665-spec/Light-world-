Ordering

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, go to checkout, enter your details and choose pickup or delivery. Your order is sent to us on WhatsApp with an order number, and we reply to confirm stock, price and delivery before anything is dispatched.

Can I order by phone or WhatsApp instead?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us on with what you need — a photo of your materials list is fine.

Do I need an account?

No. You can order without creating an account. Your cart is saved in your browser until you check out.

Can I get a proforma invoice for my client or company?

Yes. Add the items to your cart and tap Print proforma invoice on the cart page. It's valid for 7 days.

Some items say "Price on request". Why?

Prices of items such as cable rolls and appliances change with supplier and exchange rates. Tap Ask for price and we'll send today's price on WhatsApp.

Buying guides

Which cable size do I need?
  • 1.5mm — lighting circuits
  • 2.5mm — socket circuits
  • 4mm — air conditioners and heavy appliances
  • 6mm — cookers, water heaters and sub-mains

Always follow your electrician's design — when in doubt, ask us.

B22 or E27 — which bulb fits my holder?

B22 is the push-and-twist pin type; E27 is the screw type. Check your old bulb or holder before buying, or buy a matching lamp holder.

What size inverter or solar system do I need?

Add up the watts of everything you want to run at the same time. Lights, fans, TV and a fridge usually fit a 3kVA system; add an AC or pump and you'll want 5kVA. Send us your list and we'll size it for free.

Do I need a stabilizer or voltage guard?

If your area has low or unstable voltage, protect fridges, freezers and TVs with a voltage guard, or the whole house with a stabilizer.
