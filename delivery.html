How it works

From order to your door

  1. 1

    Place your order

    Check out online or send your list on WhatsApp. You get an order number straight away.

  2. 2

    We confirm

    We check stock and confirm the price, delivery cost and time with you — usually within an hour in opening hours.

  3. 3

    Items are checked and packed. Large orders get a printed invoice in the box.

  4. 4

    You receive it

    Pay on delivery in Kumasi (cash, card or MoMo), or as agreed for other areas. Check your items before the rider leaves.

Returns

Changed your mind? You can return most items within 7 days of purchase if they are:

  • unused and not installed or wired
  • in the original, undamaged packaging
  • returned with your receipt or order number

We can't take back: cable cut to length, items that have been installed, special orders made for you, and lamps or bulbs that have been used.

Bring the item to the shop, or contact us to arrange a pickup. Refunds are made by mobile money or cash within 3 working days after we inspect the item, or you can exchange it for something else.

Warranty

Many products carry a manufacturer's warranty — the period is shown in the specifications on the product page. If an item develops a fault during its warranty:

  • bring it back with your receipt or order number
  • we test it and repair or replace it, or send it to the brand's service centre
  • we keep you updated on WhatsApp

Not covered: damage from wrong installation, power surges or low voltage on unprotected appliances, water, or physical damage. Protect fridges, ACs and TVs with a voltage guard or stabilizer.

Damaged or wrong item

Please check your order when it arrives. If anything is damaged, missing or not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours with a photo on WhatsApp and we'll replace it free of charge.
